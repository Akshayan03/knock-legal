Community Rules

Last updated October 2026

Knock is for adults

You must be 18 or older. Accounts that belong to anyone younger are removed.

Be someone people want to hang out with

No harassment, threats, hate, or bullying. No sexual content or nudity on camera. No violence or anything illegal.

No spam, selling, or recruiting. Don't pretend to be someone else.

No recording

Rooms are live and are never recorded by Knock. Don't record or screenshot people without asking them.

Meeting up

Meetups are at public places only: a café, park, bar or restaurant. Never at someone's home. Tell a friend where you're going, get there on your own, and leave whenever you want.

Don't ask people where they live, and don't share where you live.

Only check in ("We met") when you actually met.

What happens when someone breaks the rules

There's zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people on Knock.

Tap a person in a room to report or block them; reporting also blocks them for you. Tap the flag on a room to report the room; it's hidden for you straight away.

Names, room titles, chat and meetup details are checked for slurs and sexual content before anyone sees them.

We review every report within 24 hours. We remove the content and pause or ban the account behind it when it breaks these rules.

When two people in a room report the same person, they're removed from that room. When three people report someone within a day, their account is paused while we review it.

Hosts can remove people from their room; removed people can't come back to it.

When three people report a room, it comes off Discover until we've looked at it.
