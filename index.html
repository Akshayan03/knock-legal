Knock

Support

Knock is live video rooms with people in your area who are into the same things, plus meetups at public places. 18+.

Contact

Email akshayanjeyakumar@gmail.com with questions, problems, safety concerns or anything you want to report. You'll hear back within 24 hours, usually much sooner.

Report someone or something

  • A person in a room: tap them, then Report. Reporting also blocks them for you. Two reports in a room remove them from it.
  • A whole room: tap the flag at the top of the room (or Report this room on its page). It's hidden for you straight away.
  • Anything else (a meetup, something that happened offline, a profile): email akshayanjeyakumar@gmail.com.

Every report is reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks the Community Rules is removed and the account behind it is paused or banned. If someone is in danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number) first.

Block someone

Tap them in a room, then Block. You won't see or hear each other in rooms, and they can't invite you. Undo it in Settings → Privacy & safety.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. It removes your profile and everything tied to it straight away. If you can't sign in any more, email akshayanjeyakumar@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

Your location

Knock never stores your position. You pick an area (like “North York”) and see live rooms from that area and the areas around it. No maps, no distances. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Video won't connect?

Video goes directly between phones. On some strict networks (some campus, office or carrier networks) a direct path isn't possible: switch between Wi-Fi and cellular and knock again.
