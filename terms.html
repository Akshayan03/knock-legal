Using Knock

Knock lets you join live video rooms with people in your area who are into the same things, play quick games, and plan meetups at public places.

You must be 18 or older and follow the Community Rules. You're responsible for what you say and show.

There's zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. We review every report within 24 hours, remove content that breaks the rules, and remove the people who post it.

Meetups

Meetups are arranged between people, not by Knock. They must be at a public place. Use your judgement.

Your account

Keep your password safe. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account; it removes your profile and everything tied to it.

We may pause or remove accounts that break the rules.

No warranty

Knock is provided as is. Live video depends on your network and may not always connect.
